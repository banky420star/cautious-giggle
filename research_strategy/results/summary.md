## How to read this

- Returns are unlevered signed exposure (1x notional), next-open fills, no stops or sizing.
- "Holdout" was never used for ranking. A strategy that tops validation but loses on holdout did not generalise.
- Walk-forward is the fairer test: it simulates re-picking the best rule as time passes.
- The cache spans roughly one year of M5 bars per symbol. One year, one broker feed and simplified rules are not enough evidence to put any of these in front of real money.
- Gaps (weekends, daily breaks) are kept as-is; an M5 bar after a gap fills at that bar's open.
